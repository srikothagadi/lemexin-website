  • Generative AI and RAG ApplicationsAssistants and search that answer from your own documents and data.

Enterprise Integration

We design and build the connections between your systems: APIs, files, events and queues. Most of our integration work is on MuleSoft and Boomi, using well-known integration patterns.

  • MuleSoft Design and DeliveryApplications, APIs and environments built to a clear standard.
  • Boomi Processes and RuntimeIntegration processes, connectors and runtime setup on Boomi.
  • API Design and GovernanceConsistent, documented APIs your teams can reuse.
  • Event-Driven and Messaging DesignsQueues and events that decouple systems and absorb spikes.
  • Error Handling and MonitoringCentral logging, alerting and retry so failures are seen and fixed.
  • Platform MigrationMoving integrations from an old platform to a new one in planned stages.
